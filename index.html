Our Philosophy

Rooted in respect for origin, refined by fire.

At Ember & Bean, we believe coffee is an agricultural wonder rather than a commodity. Every harvest tells the story of high altitudes, rich volcanic soils, and the tireless dedication of smallholder producers.

We roast exclusively in small batches using cast-iron drums. This meticulous process preserves subtle floral aromatics and fruit brightness while caramelizing the natural sugars into silky, dessert-like sweetness.

Elena Vance & Liam Moreau Head Roasters & Founders

Direct Farm Partnerships

We pay an average of 42% above Fair Trade minimums directly to cooperative farmers in Oromia, Huila, and Huehuetenango.

Precision Micro-Roasting

Continuous sensor monitoring tracks bean temperature curve to within 0.1°C, guaranteeing repeatable sweetness and body.

Zero Plastic Packaging

All beans ship in 100% home-compostable biopolymer bags with one-way plant-based degassing valves.
